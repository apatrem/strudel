Hello!

Here you have all samples from  12-bit YAMAHA RX-7 drum machine (1987).
Originally recorded at 24bit 48khz. For recording I used Lexicon mpx550 (bypass mode).
Converted to 16bit without noise dithering.


directories description:

rx7-long-decay, pitch-7	; 48khz recording of long decay samples and pitch -7, looped as originals
rx7-normal				; 48khz recording of all samples, default pitch and decay
rx7-selected				; few selected bd and snare samples with slight pitch and decay modified
rx7-upto96k  				; recorded at pitch -12, with some more decay, and then transposed from 48khz to 96khz
